Cybersecurity Rules & Regulations

Protecting your digital life begins with awareness and smart habits.

General Cybersecurity Rules

  • Use complex passwords with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Change your passwords regularly and don’t reuse them across sites.
  • Use two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
  • Think before clicking links or downloading attachments from unknown emails.
  • Keep all devices and software updated to patch security vulnerabilities.
  • Install a reliable antivirus and firewall system.
  • Be cautious while using public Wi-Fi – use a VPN if possible.
  • Do not overshare personal information on social media.
  • Verify emails that request personal info – they may be phishing scams.
  • Always backup important data to secure storage/cloud services.

Common Types of Cybercrime

  • Phishing: Fake emails or messages that trick you into revealing sensitive information.
  • Ransomware: Malicious software that locks your files until you pay money.
  • Identity Theft: When someone steals your personal data to impersonate you.
  • Cyberbullying: Using digital platforms to harass or threaten someone.
  • Data Breaches: Unauthorized access and theft of sensitive data from systems.
  • Online Scams: Fraudulent schemes like fake job offers or lottery wins.
  • Hacking: Breaking into computer systems without permission.

Recent Cybersecurity News

  • Global: In 2024, a ransomware attack hit over 100 hospitals in the U.S., locking patient data and demanding millions in ransom.
  • Nepal: In early 2025, the Nepal Rastra Bank reported attempted phishing attacks targeting digital wallets and banking apps.
  • Education: Students in multiple countries were targeted through fake scholarship and exam portals.
  • Social Media: Over 50,000 Instagram accounts were hacked in a credential-stuffing attack in late 2024.

Cybercrime is increasing globally. Stay alert and report incidents to Nepal Police Cyber Bureau.

Nepal's Cyber Laws

  • Electronic Transactions Act (ETA), 2063: Main law to prevent and punish cybercrime in Nepal.
  • Cybercrime Investigation: Conducted by the Cyber Bureau of Nepal Police.
  • Unauthorized access, hacking, and spreading viruses are criminal offenses.
  • Online defamation, hate speech, and cyberbullying are punishable under the Act.
  • Sharing obscene content or fake news online can lead to imprisonment and fines.
  • Online financial fraud and identity theft are strictly prohibited.

Visit the Nepal Cyber Bureau to learn more or report incidents.
