Introduction to Cybersecurity

Understanding digital threats and how to stay safe online.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity refers to the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from digital attacks. It involves implementing measures to secure computer systems from unauthorized access, damage, or theft. As our dependency on digital technology increases, ensuring cybersecurity is essential for personal privacy, business integrity, and national security.

From individuals to governments, everyone needs to understand the fundamentals of cybersecurity to navigate the digital world safely.

What are Cyber Attacks?

Cyber attacks are malicious attempts by individuals or groups to breach digital systems. These attacks aim to steal, alter, or destroy data, disrupt services, or gain unauthorized access. Common types include malware, phishing, denial-of-service attacks, and more.

These threats are evolving, making it essential for users to stay informed and prepared.

Precautions to Stay Safe

  • Use strong, unique passwords and change them regularly.
  • Enable two-factor authentication on all important accounts.
  • Install and update antivirus and anti-malware software.
  • Keep your operating system and software updated.
  • Avoid clicking on suspicious links or email attachments.
  • Use secure connections, especially when accessing sensitive information.
  • Regularly back up your important files.
  • Educate yourself and others about cybersecurity best practices.
